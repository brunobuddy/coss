The CLI manages what it could only read. New: `coss suppressions`, `coss subaccounts`, `coss log-drains`, `coss audiences`, `coss segments`, `coss topics`, `coss marketing settings`, `coss risk rules`, `coss risk check`, `coss risk classify`, `coss risk settings`, `coss sign settings` and `coss scheduling schedules`; `coss emails cancel` and `reschedule`, `coss sms cancel`, `coss sms suppressions check`, `coss contacts update`, `import` and `export`, `coss broadcasts update`, `remove`, `pause` and `resume`, `coss forms update` and `remove`, `coss sign templates create`, and `coss scheduling hosts create`, `update` and `remove`, `event-types create` and `update`, `book`, `reschedule`, `approve` and `decline`. `coss open` knows every page of the dashboard, and unknown commands point at `coss api`.
