Scheduling's own OAuth apps: `coss.scheduling.oauthApps` connects hosts' calendars through your own Google, Microsoft or Zoom app, so the consent screen shows your brand. Calendar connections gain `oauth_app`, settings gain `privacy_url`, and there are `scheduling.oauth_app.failing` and `.restored` webhooks. `coss open scheduling-oauth-apps` opens its dashboard page.
