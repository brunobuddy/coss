Verify's settings have an API: `coss.verify.settings.get()` and `.update()`. The MCP server gets tools for marketing (contacts, audiences, segments, topics, broadcasts), Verify and Sign, and more for Forms, Scheduling, Hooks and Risk, plus `COSS_MCP_TOOLSETS` to offer fewer of them. The CLI gets `coss verify`, `coss contacts`, `coss broadcasts` and `coss sign`, and more of `coss sms`, `coss forms` and `coss scheduling`.
