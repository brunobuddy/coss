---
title: Testing
description: "Try Sign in a sandbox environment before anyone signs for real: requests work end to end on the real signing page, with a few differences."
---

Requests made with a sandbox (`coss_test_…`) key work end to end: sessions open the real signing
page, signers sign, and the request is sealed and completed. What's different:

- **No emails reach anyone.** Invitations, reminders and copies go through the sandbox's fake
  provider: read them in the dashboard's emails page instead.
- **The signing page says it's a test** ("signing it has no legal effect"), and so does the
  certificate.
- **The seal is a test seal.** The completed PDF is sealed and verifies, but with a self-signed
  certificate ("coss.com Sign (sandbox test seal)"), so PDF readers like Acrobat show the signature
  as not trusted. Live requests are sealed with coss.com's trusted certificate.
- **Nothing is billed**, and sandbox requests don't count against the Free plan's requests.
- **`allowed_origins` can include `http://localhost`** for embedding while you develop.

Test PDFs need nothing special; put anchors like `{{signature:signer_1}}` in them to place fields
without coordinates. Webhooks (`sign_request.*`) are delivered as in live environments, and
`coss trigger sign_request.completed` sends a sample one.
