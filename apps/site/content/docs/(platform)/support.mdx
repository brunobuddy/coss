Every ticket is read and answered by a person. You can reach us two ways, and they're the same
conversation: a reply in one shows up in the other.

- **From the dashboard:** open **Support** in your organization's sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘K</kbd>
  and search for it.
- **By email:** write to [support@coss.com](mailto:support@coss.com).

## Raising a ticket

Say what happened, what you expected, and any ids or error messages you saw (an email id or a
request's `X-Request-Id` is the fastest way for us to find it). Please leave out passwords and API
keys. The form tells you when to expect a reply before you send it.

A ticket raised from the dashboard belongs to that organization. Owners and admins see all of an
organization's tickets; other members see their own.

## Emailing us

Email us from the address on your coss.com account. If we can prove the message really came from
that address and you belong to one organization, the ticket appears in that organization's
dashboard and follows its plan. Your domain needs to publish a DMARC policy for us to prove that.

Otherwise we can't tell who sent it, since anyone can put any address in the From line. We still
read and answer it, but a person checks who you are before it joins your organization, and we won't
share account details until then. A ticket raised from the dashboard never has this problem.

## What happens next

You get a confirmation with your ticket number and when to expect a reply. Replies arrive by email
and in the dashboard, and you can answer in either. Replying to a resolved ticket opens it again.

If we ask you something and don't hear back for a week, we close the ticket with a note; replying
opens it again. You can also mark a ticket as resolved yourself.
